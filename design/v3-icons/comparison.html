Why these colours

Ember#FFC24B → #F4573E

Amber into coral-red. The warm end of the spectrum — reads as memory, film and light rather than 'AI'. Raycast proved a single coral-red carries a whole near-black app; this is that move with a sunrise gradient instead of a flat fill.

Verdant#93E9AC → #12B981

Mint into emerald. Green is the honest colour of 'verified, nothing lost, backup done', so it pairs with the product's integrity promise. Cool and grown-up, with none of the indigo sheen that reads as generic AI.

Marine#8FC2FF → #2E6BE6

Sky into royal blue. The safe, pro choice — but a true royal blue, not the cyan-teal of the old mark and not an electric indigo. Calm, tool-like, sits easily in a Dock of Apple icons.

Citrine#E8F87A → #7BD34E

Chartreuse into leaf green. The most distinctive direction — the accent family Arc, Supabase and Vercel use to look crafted rather than corporate. High energy on near-black; unmistakably not an AI-gradient app.

Apple's 2025 Liquid Glass guidance asks for bold weights over hairline strokes and coloured backgrounds, so every icon keeps the brand squircle at #0b0b0d and uses a ~63px gradient stroke with a soft accent glow and a lit core. Marks are drawn on the same 24-unit grid as the current icon, so any of them drops straight into src-tauri/icons/.

Why these four

Ember

Amber into coral-red. Reads as film, light and memory rather than software demo. Raycast proved a single coral-red carries a whole near-black app; this is that move with a sunrise gradient instead of a flat fill.

Verdant

Mint into emerald. Green is the honest colour of verified, nothing lost, backup done, so it matches what the product actually promises. No indigo sheen anywhere.

Marine

Sky into true royal blue. The safe, pro choice, and a real blue rather than the cyan-teal the old mark used. Calm and tool-like; sits quietly among Apple icons.

Citrine

Chartreuse into leaf green. The most distinctive direction: the accent family Arc, Supabase and Vercel use to look crafted instead of corporate. High energy on near-black.

Every mark is drawn on the same 24-unit grid with round caps and a 1.9px stroke, so it stays legible at menu-bar size. The icon keeps the #0b0b0d squircle with a gradient stroke, a soft glow and a lit core, which is what Apple's 2025 guidance asks for.

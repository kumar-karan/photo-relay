Photo Relay — icon exploration

Moving Photo Relay away from the default-AI look (purple/indigo aperture, glow). Ten geometric marks for the same product thought — photos relayed iPhone → Mac → Samsung — drawn in one recommended palette and tested for real at 16/32/64px.

What reads crafted vs default-AI

The AI tellIndigo and violet (#6366f1, #8b5cf6, #7c3aed) and purple→cyan gradients. The current mark (#7d6cff → #40c6dd) is a textbook sample. The 2025–26 anti-slop consensus names indigo the #1 fingerprint.
One hue, done wellRaycast: near-black #07080a + one brand red #ff6363, everything else greyscale. Crafted icons ration colour; they don't gradient it.
Warm = physicalArc sunrise yellow, Pixelmator Pro sunset orange, Bear amber, Camo lime, Things' flat reds. Warm/light temperatures read as physical objects; violet reads as software default.
Geometry over glowDistinctive profiles at 16px come from solid stops and weight contrast, not halos. Align glyphs to a 24-grid, keep strokes ≥2 units, let one solid shape anchor each mark.

Three palettes

Recommended

Ember Signal

#ffc46b, #ff8a1e, #f0420b, #7a3f15, #0b0b0d

Warm light for a photo relay: amber source, orange core, burnt-ember depth.

Photos are light, and a relay is light travelling between two devices. Ember is a warm, physical family: Arc's sunrise yellow, Pixelmator Pro's sunset orange, Bear's amber, Things' warmth all read as crafted light, never as default-AI. It keeps Raycast's discipline of one brand hue doing all the work on near-black. Contrast on #0b0b0d is strong (~11:1 at 16px) because amber carries luminance as well as hue, and it sits at the opposite end of the spectrum from the AI purple every icon explorer starts from.

Alternative

Lime Instrument

#eaf9a9, #d2ec4b, #18a881, #335e3f, #0b0b0d

Chartreuse-to-teal, a precision instrument panel.

Green family chosen for instrument energy, not nature: Camo's lime, Linear's green accents, GrapheneOS chartreuse. Reads precise, reliable, 'transfer complete'. Problem: green is also the universal success state, so it can read as status rather than identity, and it edges toward the same saturated-futuristic register people already associate with AI.

Alternative

Studio Ivory

#f1eae0, #cfc5b6, #f97316, #51493f, #0b0b0d

Warm monochrome, one chrome-orange punctuation point.

Things / Notion minimalism: the mark is warm ivory on near-black at near-maximum luminance contrast, so it is the most legible of the three at 16px, and one strong orange point (the 'live' node) carries the brand. The most reserved and timeless of the three; the risk is that with no colour doing the job, the icon depends entirely on the geometry, and it can read plain rather than distinctive at Dock size.
